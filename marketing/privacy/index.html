Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Signal Shift (“the game”) is a single-player puzzle game for iPhone and Android, published by Techbolic (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the game handles.

In short: we run no accounts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. The only network activity is advertising, provided by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked and completed levels, stars, best move counts, daily puzzle completions, hint balance) and your settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced motion) in a file on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not linked to any account, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Data we collect

Signal Shift itself collects no personal data. It has no user accounts and no analytics of its own. The only network activity is the advertising described below.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • optional rewarded videos that play when you choose to watch one for a hint, and
  • for players without the Supporter Pass, an occasional unrewarded ad break between puzzles.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. See Google’s explanation at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent first. The Supporter Pass removes ad breaks.

Purchases

Optional purchases (puzzle packs and the Supporter Pass) are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which products you own.

Children

The game contains no violence or mature content, but it is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Withdraw or change ad consent where the game offers it.
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@techbolic.com.
